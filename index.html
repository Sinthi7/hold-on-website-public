The journey

A Journey Through Time

Twelve epochs. One finger. How far through time can you hold on?

  1. 01 Primordial Ocean

    Sulphur bubbles rise through the first dark waters.

  2. 02 First Cells

    Life begins small: soft amoebas wander through the water.

  3. 03 Life in the Sea

    Jellyfish, predatory fish and puffers fill the deep.

  4. 04 The Land Turns Green

    Leaves drift and giant dragonflies whir over the first green land.

  5. 05 Age of the Dinosaurs

    Raptors race and pterosaurs circle through the dusty air.

  6. 06 Ice Age

    Snow falls, snowballs roll and frost bats beat their wings.

  7. 07 The First Humans

    Embers glow above campfires as boulders roll past.

  8. 08 The First Cities

    Lit windows rise into the night, watched by stone guardians.

  9. 09 The Future

    Robots and drones patrol a bright, electric tomorrow.

  10. 10 Spaceflight

    Among the stars, asteroids tumble and satellites turn.

  11. 11 The Stars Fade

    The last stars glow red as restless energy pulses in the dark.

  12. 12 End of the Universe

    Reality cracks open, and nothing holds its shape.

About the game

Hold on. Don’t let go.

HOLD ON: Through Time is a one-finger survival game. Place your finger on the screen and keep it there — every movement guides you, every release ends the run.

Your path leads through different ages of time. Stay focused, stay calm, and see how far your journey takes you.
