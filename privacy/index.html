2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the HOLD ON: Through Time app for iOS and to the official HOLD ON website.

The website may be published before the app is available. All information about the app refers to the version of the app published in the App Store. Until the app has been published, the descriptions of the app do not yet apply.

HOLD ON does not have its own user accounts and does not operate its own server for game data.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

HOLD ON stores certain game information locally on your device:

  • Your high score
  • Collected coins
  • Your settings for music, sound effects and haptics
  • A score that has not yet been confirmed by Game Center, so that it can be submitted later

This information is stored on your device and is not sent to a server operated by us.

Deleting the app generally removes locally stored game information, subject to your device’s backup and restoration settings.

4. Apple Game Center

HOLD ON uses Apple Game Center to provide a global leaderboard.

When you use Game Center, Apple may process information such as your Game Center account details, player name, leaderboard scores and profile information. When a run ends, HOLD ON submits your score to the leaderboard if you are signed in to Game Center.

Your Game Center player name and leaderboard score may be visible to other players according to Apple’s Game Center settings. HOLD ON only reads your player name to display Game Center features in the app and does not store it.

Game Center is operated by Apple. We do not operate our own Game Center database.

For more information, please consult Apple’s privacy information on Game Center and the privacy settings on your Apple device.

5. Optional Rewarded Advertisements

HOLD ON offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob.

You may choose to watch an advertisement to receive an additional opportunity to continue playing.

Watching an advertisement is voluntary. You can play HOLD ON without watching advertisements.

Google’s advertising services are only started when you choose to watch an advertisement for the first time.

When advertisements are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may process information including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising interactions and related information
  • Approximate location, such as information inferred from an IP address
  • App interaction information
  • Performance and diagnostic information
  • Crash information

Depending on the applicable permissions, consent choices, settings and advertising services involved, this information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and other purposes described in Google’s privacy documentation.

Where the required consents and permissions have been given, Google may also use device identifiers for tracking, meaning that information from this app may be linked with information from apps and websites of other companies for advertising purposes.

We do not operate our own advertising tracking system.

For more information, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and the information on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Privacy Choices: Google Consent and Apple Tracking Permission

Where required, HOLD ON uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices relating to advertising.

To determine which privacy choices apply in your region and to provide them, the UMP processes certain technical information, such as approximate location inferred from your IP address and information about your interaction with the privacy messages. Your choices are stored on your device.

You can reopen available Google privacy choices through the Privacy Options section in the game’s settings.

HOLD ON also uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission system.

If iOS permits the request, the ATT prompt may appear when you first choose to watch a rewarded advertisement.

You may allow or deny tracking permission. Your choice does not prevent you from playing HOLD ON or using the game’s coin-based continue feature.

Rewarded advertisements may also be available if you deny tracking permission, depending on consent requirements and advertisement availability.

Denying ATT permission prevents access to Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. It does not necessarily prevent Google from processing other information needed for permitted advertising operations.

You can manage tracking permissions in your iPhone’s privacy settings.

7. Website and Hosting

The official address of the HOLD ON website is hold-on.ch. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc.

The website does not use its own analytics service, advertising trackers or tracking cookies, and it does not load fonts, scripts or other content from third-party services.

When you visit the website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, request details and browser information to deliver the website and maintain security.

If you choose a language using the language switch, your choice is stored only in your browser’s local storage so that the website can be shown in your preferred language on later visits. It is not sent to us or to third parties and is not used for tracking. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

The website offers a playable browser demo of HOLD ON at hold-on.ch/demo/. The demo stores your best score, collected coins and your settings for music, sound effects and haptics only in your browser’s local storage, so that they are still available on later visits. This information is not sent to us or to third parties and is not used for tracking. The demo contains no advertising and does not use Game Center. You can delete this information at any time in your browser settings.

The Privacy Policy link in the app opens this website in an in-app browser view.

Please consult the GitHub General Privacy Statement for further information.

8. Legal Bases

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal data is processed on the following legal bases:

  • Advertising: Where consent is required, the processing of personal data for advertising and the storing of or access to information on your device are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Where Google relies on other legal bases for certain purposes, such as legitimate interests, this is described in Google’s privacy documentation and in the available privacy choices.
  • Privacy choices: Providing and storing the required privacy choices is based on our legitimate interest in meeting applicable consent requirements (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where applicable, on legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Game Center leaderboard: Submitting your score is based on our legitimate interest in offering a leaderboard (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Website: Delivering the website securely is based on our legitimate interest in a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Storing your selected language is based on our legitimate interest in showing the website in your preferred language (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Storing the browser demo’s best score, coins and settings in your browser is based on our legitimate interest in offering a demo that keeps your progress and settings (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Contact: Answering your messages is based on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where you exercise your privacy rights, on our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example through the Privacy Options section in the game’s settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection does not generally require a legal basis for processing. We process personal data in accordance with its principles.
